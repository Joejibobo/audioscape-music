Preview: 

A light theme with 16-bit RPGs in mind, weaving gentle but firm mid and high tones. Suggested uses include stuff like a crystal dungeon, waterfall scene, peaceful village, gazing at a starry night, or relaxing to.

RECOMMENDED: Version 1.2 is the newest revision and the most "complete". If you'd prefer it without flute, grab version 1.1. If you're specifically aiming for a thinner sound without flute or vibraphone, the original "Crystal Dungeon" is for you.
