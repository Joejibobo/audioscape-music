🌌🐱🌌 The original soundtrack of the game jam winner, then loser. 🌌🐱🌌

The soundtrack for the video game, "Space Captain Yanya", a little space exploration and monster collecting game made for the "OpenGameArt Late Fall Game Jam 2021".

I tried to be quite subtle and understated in this one, and make music that was suitable for use within the game, not for listening by itself or to be impressive to listen to, so it might sound a bit simple, but it worked very well within the game itself :3c and its still fairly enjoyable :3c
