Preview: 

I just finished this album, consisting of moderately paced EDM inspired by the worlds of video games. Since it is inspired by video games, I thought I might publish it here as well as Bandcamp.

The preview song is track 2 - "Quantum."

I don't remember sampling or copying from anyone else, but the equipment that you can hear includes a Korg Minilogue, Korg Volca Drum, Behringer OD400 Overdrive/Distortion, and TC Electronic Hall of Fame Reverb. Credit to me (Ivan Stanton) is not required, but I do appreciate it.

Bandcamp link if you want other formats: https://ivanastan.bandcamp.com/album/the-epic-adventure

Tracklist:

  1. Initd - a company logo, or console startup theme
  2. Quantum - the first level of a platformer
  3. Worlds Beyond - underwater/underground theme
  4. The Forest Fortress - a castle theme, but a bit more fun than that
  5. Negative Altitude - underwater/underground theme
  6. The Orchestra in Heaven - sky theme
  7. The Dark Planet - final level
  8. The Great Battle - battle theme
  9. Photons - credits theme
