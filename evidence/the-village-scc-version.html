Simple style perfect as the soundtrack for a retro video game. Catchy tunes in the style of games that we liked in the early 90s but with sounds that sound more current and more intensely. I was inspired by songs game 8/16 Bits. Perfect for flash games, browser games, or games in general who want to adopt a classic.
