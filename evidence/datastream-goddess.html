Preview: 

An upbeat, energetic, and danceable track inspired by COMPILE shooters from the 80's and 90's. Good for action scenes, particularly shoot-em-ups, or positive experiences perhaps involving computers or an AI.

PLEASE NOTE: v1.11 are the newest versions, which I'd recommend in most cases! The piano accompanied version is the one currently in the preview, though and there is also an alternate Casio synth string accompanied version (without piano). The original is still available below as well for those who want it.
