This is just a cute little loop, reminicist of tracks by Snail's House or Masashi Kageyama's soundtrack for Mr. Gimmick. If you like what I did, you should defintly check out these two artists for some cozy goodness (heck, even if you didn't my track, they are defintly worth a shot).
If you wanna check out more of my stuff, visit me on Youtube! https://www.youtube.com/user/Weltenmaler

If you're planning on using it in something, please leave me a comment. It's not a must, but i'm curious, where my stuff gets used :) In case you need a longer loop, drop me a pm and I'll send you something.

 

Orchestral samples included in this recording from the Vienna Symphonic Library.
