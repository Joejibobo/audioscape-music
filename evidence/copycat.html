Preview: 

Have you ever heard of the famous meme "nyancat" ? The song featured in nyancat is composed by daniwell, an awesome Japanese composer with a unique style. I attempted to imitate his bouncy, upbeat style here. 

I'm not sure how useful this piece may be in games, but I hope it may serve useful in some way. Thanks for listening! 

 

update: i've added a revised version which contains fixed/improved chords and notes, a little synth modification and a more comfortable volume ( i think), which should provide a better audio experience. i hope you'll agree. thanks! 08/11/14

you can also download this from my new album: https://greenbearmusic.bandcamp.com/album/bgm-fun-vol-5
