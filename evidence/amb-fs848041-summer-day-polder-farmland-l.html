At this ditch side with trees and shrubs in this polder-land, many honeybees fly around. Birds sing and a friendly breeze blows.

About the picture
The photograph depicts a tranquil polder landscape on a sunny day, with a narrow asphalt path winding through an open green plain. To the left of the path are a few tall trees with light, open crowns, while to the right, denser shrubbery and low vegetation run along the edge of the path. In the foreground, a microphone on a tripod stands in the grass, clearly set up to capture ambient sounds. Further away, behind the bushes and between the green edges of the landscape, a farmhouse is also visible. It lies low in the landscape, half hidden behind the greenery, blending beautifully into the peaceful, rural surroundings. On the horizon, behind the village of Lage-Zwaluwe, several wind turbines and a row of trees complete the picture.

Note on the honeybees.
About 1.5 km north of here, across the river, is the protected nature reserve "De Biesbosch" which is home to many wild bees.
There are 11 commercial amateur hives here. In a census we did last year we counted about 40 hives in this small part of this polder.
Around the nature reserve, according to the newspaper, there must be hundreds of hives.
Because these honey bees we fly 5 km for food they are now a (too) big competition for the wild bees in the nature reserve. This is more than all annoying, but the wildlife managers in the nature reserve are powerless. After all, they are not prohibited from placing the hives.
