Preview: 

This is a cute and playful sounding track. What you are hearing is the extended version of this. I have a short version and a "pocket edition" which are more ideal for in-game looping. This track would also work well, but there are some musical transitions that might not compliment a game as well if it's only being used as background music. Check out the link below to find the other versions: http://www.matthewpablo.com/archives/new-game-music-10-16-13

I hope you guys enjoy this track.

Also, if needed I can re-arrange it to best suit your needs.

There are those who might want re-arrangements of this track tailored specific to their project, this is also another good reason to communicate with the author.
