Funny sounding acoustic + electronic experimental piece. It starts mid-paced and eventually transforms into an fast-paced thing.

Some of the people who have listened to it have told me it would be suitable for a puzzle game.

Instruments: Piano, acoustic drums, bass, guitars, synths.

Enjoy.
